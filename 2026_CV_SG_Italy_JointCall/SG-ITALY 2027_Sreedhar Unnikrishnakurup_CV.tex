\section{7. Research interests}
Physics-informed artificial intelligence and deep learning for automated defect detection, sizing, and confidence estimation in non-destructive testing (NDT); active infrared thermography combining electromagnetic induction and optical (flash/pulsed, lock-in, and laser) excitation for polymer matrix composites (CFRP/GFRP), curved aerospace structures, and wind turbine blades; multiphysics finite element modeling (FEM) of electromagnetic-thermal and optical heat transfer in anisotropic composites to generate synthetic training datasets; automated, in-line, and robot-assisted thermographic inspection systems with multi-sensor data fusion and 3D defect mapping; and multimodal NDE validation using ultrasonic testing (UT), X-ray computed tomography (CT), shearography, and terahertz/hyperspectral imaging.
