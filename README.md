## About Me

I am a **Senior Scientist** in the Structural Materials Department at the **Institute of Materials Research and Engineering (IMRE), A\*STAR**, Singapore. With over **11 years of post-Ph.D. research experience** across Asia and Europe (IMRE A\*STAR, Politecnico di Bari, IIT Madras, BAM Berlin, and ZUT Poland), my research focuses on quantitative **Non-Destructive Testing & Evaluation (NDT&E)**, **Infrared Thermography (Pulsed, Lock-in, Induction, and Laser-based IRT)**, **AI/ML-driven defect recognition**, and **multiphysics computational modeling** of advanced structural materials and composites.

### Key Highlights
- **Research Leadership:** PI and Co-PI on competitive national and multinational R&D programs (including the Singapore Aerospace Programme, Polymer Matrix Composites Programme, and AI Singapore's MIDGAI).
- **Publications & Impact:** **20 peer-reviewed journal articles** (including *Nature Energy*, *NDT & E International*, *Composites Part B*, *Composite Structures*, and *Int. J. Thermal Sciences*) and **37 conference papers** (**H-index: 12**).
- **Technology Translation:** **9 Technology Disclosures (TDs)** filed at A\*STAR (2021–2025), with **1 TD licensed** to industry for composite inspection workflows.
- **Professional Credentials:** Certified **Level 1 Infrared Thermographer** (ITC), **QIRT-Asia Steering Committee Member**, Chartered Engineer (IEI), and Member of NDTSS & ISNT.
